## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the grounded assurance and natural warmth of modern Scandinavian product design. Built on principles of organic restraint, soft linen surfaces, and artisanal quietude, the visual environment feels human, tactile, and deeply dependable—evoking the serene confidence of a thoughtfully designed Swedish home rather than a cold enterprise portal or generic SaaS template.

The style harmonizes soft-warm minimalism with delicate tactile elevation. It rejects harsh stark whites and sterile grays in favor of unbleached linen, warm limestone, deep pine slate, and muted botanical greens. Visual depth is established through tonal layering, generous breathing room, ultra-subtle atmospheric shadows, and soft micro-borders that evoke fine-grain paper and brushed natural materials.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in muted Nordic landscapes: deep spruce slate, coastal pine waters, weathered lichen, and warm sheep’s wool.

- **Primary (`#183335`):** A dense slate-pine serving as the dominant typographic anchor and primary action surface. It gives authoritative weight without the harsh detachment of pure black.
- **Secondary (`#36736B`):** A serene sea-pine green used for focal accents, secondary interactions, badge surfaces, and subtle interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#A8C99B`):** A muted sage that functions as an organic highlight, positive feedback indicator, and soft tint for icon containers.
- **Neutral (`#FAFAF5`):** An organic, light-cream linen tone that serves as the canvas foundation, eliminating digital glare while lending warmth and natural depth.
- **Meadow Accent (`#8BCB75`):** A vibrant yet natural leaf green reserved strictly for micro-moments of delight, key conversion badges, and energetic status accents.
- **Surface & Border Tokens:** Elevated cards use pure `#FFFFFF` against `#FAFAF5` or `#F4F3EB` backgrounds, separated only by whisper-quiet border strokes (`#E7E5DB`) that replicate high-end paper stationery edges.

## Typography

Typography relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve the soft, rounded, humanist geometric cadence reminiscent of contemporary Nordic lifestyle editorial work and circular typography. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Set with tight tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) and balanced line heights. They convey warmth and clarity without aggressive display quirks. On mobile, display scales compress proportionally to preserve natural sentence cadence without awkward hyphenation or edge crowding.
- **Body:** Given generous line heights (`1.6` to `1.75`) to encourage unhurried reading and maximum legibility across sustained interaction paths.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Retain medium and semi-bold weights to guarantee optical balance alongside icons, inputs, and badge tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed around an open, airy 12-column fluid grid system bounded by generous content wells (maximum container width: `1200px`). White space is treated as an active material—a structural pause that imbues the interface with calm confidence.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2.5rem`.
  - **Tablet (640px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`.
  - **Mobile (< 640px):** 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.25rem`.

Card groups and form stacks maintain wide vertical gaps (`space-xl`), ensuring no cluster feels constrained. Inner card padding defaults to `space-xl` on desktop and steps down to `space-lg` on mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through soft tonal layering, delicate bone borders, and ultra-diffuse warm ambient shadows. Heavy dropshadows and glossy artificial highlights are avoided.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** Base background `#FAFAF5` (warm linen).
  - **Level 1 (Card / Surface):** Crisp white `#FFFFFF` or pale sandstone `#F4F3EB` paired with a 1px continuous stroke in `#E7E5DB`.
  - **Level 2 (Float / Dropdown / Modal):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ambient tinted shadow: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(24, 51, 53, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(24, 51, 53, 0.03)`.
- **Micro-Borders:** Boundaries rely on soft outlines (`#E7E5DB`) rather than pure contrast breaks. This creates a tactile, physical feel akin to stacked, high-grade stationery cards.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is defined by friendly, organic curvature that avoids industrial severity while steering clear of overly toy-like circular motifs:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Chips):** `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for a natural, comforting grip.
- **Structural Containers (Cards, Sheet Panels, Modals):** `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) up to `rounded-2xl` (2rem / 32px), creating soft, organic framing around internal content.
- **Pill Tags & Status Badges:** Fully circular (`rounded-full`) pill caps to provide quick graphical contrast against larger structural cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep slate background (`#183335`), pure white text, subtle hover lift to `#234547`, corner radius `0.75rem` (12px), vertical padding `0.875rem`, horizontal padding `1.75rem`.
- **Secondary:** Warm cream surface (`#F4F3EB`), deep pine text (`#183335`), 1px border `#E7E5DB`. On hover, background shifts to `#EAE7DC`.
- **Tertiary / Subtle:** Transparent background, slate text, minimal underline or background wash on hover.

### Cards
- **Structure:** Clean white surface (`#FFFFFF`), outer border 1px solid `#E7E5DB`, border radius `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Padding:** `2rem` desktop, `1.25rem` mobile.
- **Elevation:** Subtle ambient drop `0 4px 20px rgba(24, 51, 53, 0.04)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Delicate tinted background (e.g., `#A8C99B` at 20% opacity or `#8BCB75` at 15%), text `#183335`, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.35rem 0.85rem`.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill shape with border `#E7E5DB`, resting on `#FFFFFF`. Active state inverts to `#183335` background with white text.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- **Container:** Warm neutral fill (`#FFFFFF`), border 1px solid `#D8D5C8`, rounded radius `0.75rem` (12px), height `3.25rem`, padding `0 1rem`.
- **Focus State:** Stroke sharpens to `#36736B` with a gentle 3px ring in `rgba(54, 115, 107, 0.15)`. No raw blue or default browser outlines.
- **Placeholder:** Calibrated neutral `#8C9797` for soft, clear direction.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded squares (6px radius) with a 1.5px border `#D8D5C8`. Selected state fills with `#183335` and displays a crisp white checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Concentric ring design with `#183335` fill on active state.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Items separated by clean 1px hairline rules in `#E7E5DB`.
- Interactive rows feature a gentle background transition to `#F4F3EB` with `0.5rem` internal corner smoothing.
